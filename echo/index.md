# Privacy Policy — Echo Music Player

**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

**Developer:** KaKooApps  
**App:** Echo - Music Player  
**Package:** com.kakoo.apps.echo  
**Contact:** kakoo.apps@gmail.com

## Overview

Echo is an offline-first music player designed to play audio files stored locally on your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data (if any) is collected and how it is used.

---

## Data We Do NOT Collect

**Echo does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.**

Specifically:

- **No analytics or tracking SDKs** are included (Firebase Analytics is not active).
- **No crash reporting services** are integrated.
- **No network requests** are made by the app. The app has no internet-dependent functionality.
- **No user accounts** are required or supported.
- **No advertising SDKs** are included.
- **No location data** is collected.
- **No personal information** (name, email, phone number) is collected.

---

## Data Stored on Device

The following data is stored **locally on your device only** and is never transmitted:

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Playback preferences (shuffle, repeat mode, volume) | Remember your settings between sessions | Android DataStore (app-private) |
| Sleep timer settings | Auto-stop playback after set duration | Android DataStore (app-private) |
| Equalizer settings | Custom audio preferences | Android DataStore (app-private) |
| Recently played history | Show recently played songs in-app | Room database (local only) |
| Playlist data you create | Store your custom playlists | Room database (local only) |

All locally stored data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

---

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|------------|----------------|
| **READ_MEDIA_AUDIO** (Android 13+) | Read audio files from your device storage to build your music library |
| **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE** (Android 12 and below) | Read audio files from your device storage |
| **FOREGROUND_SERVICE** | Keep music playing in the background |
| **FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK** | Required for media playback foreground service on Android 14+ |
| **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED** | Restore playback state after device reboot (optional) |

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data. All audio files remain on your device.

## Children's Privacy

Echo is rated for Everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information from anyone, including children.

---

## Data Retention

- **On-device data** (playback preferences, playlists, history) is retained until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
- No data is retained by any third party.

---

## Security

All data is stored in Android's private app sandbox, accessible only to the Echo app on your device. Local app storage is encrypted by Android's file-based encryption on supported devices.
